About the Department

Why we serve.

Flaming Security is the volunteer security department of the Young Ministers' Retreat — built to protect people so that ministry can happen without fear.

Our Mandate

Security as an act of service.

Every YMR gathering brings ministers, guests and thousands of attendees into one space. Flaming Security exists to make that space safe — scouting venues before anyone else arrives, holding the perimeter during the event, and following up with every volunteer after it ends. We are now in our sixth year of structured induction training and deployment.

We operate on a simple principle: firmness paired with courtesy. Every attendee is treated with dignity. Every post is held until properly relieved. Every incident is reported up the chain, not sat on until convenient.

Pastor Daniel Olawande — known widely as P. Daniel — is a minister, songwriter, revivalist, pastor and youth leader born on August 8, 1991, in Ekiti State, Nigeria. He grew up in a devout Christian family and pursued his education in Biology Education and Educational Psychology.

Code of Conduct

What every volunteer commits to.

From the moment you're deployed to the moment you're stood down, these standards are non-negotiable — for General Personnel, Unit Heads and command officers alike.

Punctuality & Uniform

Report at least 20–30 minutes before call-up in full, clean, pressed uniform. Late arrivals are excluded from that session's service. Incorrect uniform = same consequence.

Post Discipline

No post may be left without a formal handover approved by a Unit Head or Assistant Unit Head. Abandoning post without authorization is a serious breach.

Incident Reporting

Incidents are escalated immediately through the chain of command. Nothing is held until after the event. If in doubt, report up — do not make unilateral decisions on the floor.

Zero Tolerance Items

Weapons, alcohol, drugs, explosives and unauthorized recording gear are banned for all personnel — including any attached military or paramilitary staff — without exception.

Attendee Dignity

Every attendee is treated with courtesy and respect. Harassment — physical, verbal or otherwise — of any attendee or fellow volunteer results in immediate removal from the department.

Confidentiality

Internal matters — personnel decisions, incident details, command communications — stay internal. Disclosure to outsiders or social media without authorization is a disciplinary matter.

Chain of Command

The chain of command is respected regardless of a volunteer's personal status or seniority outside the department. Only authorized officers may give orders on the field.

Equipment Accountability

All issued gear is your responsibility. Damage or loss in your care is paid for by you — repair cost for repairable items, replacement cost for total losses. See the Equipment Policy page.

Christian Character On and Off Post

Volunteers represent the ministry — not only during the event. Conduct unbecoming of a Christian minister before, during or after deployment is subject to review.

Breaches & Consequences

Any breach of this Code of Conduct is reported to the Unit Head and escalated to the HOD. Depending on severity, consequences range from a formal warning and exclusion from that session's service, to suspension from the department for a full cycle, to permanent removal. The chain of command — not individual officers — determines outcomes. The All Personnel Unit leads investigation alongside CI before a matter is brought to a final decision.
