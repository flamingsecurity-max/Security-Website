Who We Are

A department, not a rota.

Flaming Security exists to secure every YMR gathering — from the moment a venue is scouted to the last volunteer's safe return home — so that ministry can happen without fear. We hold ourselves to a standard built on four values.

01

Excellence

Every post, every patrol, done to the same standard — whether ten people are watching or none.

02

Integrity

Confidentiality, honesty and consistency, on duty and off it.

03

Vigilance

Situational awareness before, during and after every event — nothing is "someone else's zone."

04

Christian Character

Firmness paired with courtesy. Every attendee treated with dignity, every time.

Structure

Eight units. One operation.

Every unit has a clearly defined mandate and reports through the same chain of command. Explore the full KPI breakdown for each unit on the Units & KPIs page.

Intelligence

Central Intelligence

Risk assessment, VIP protection, incident investigation and de-escalation.

Frontline

Advance Team

Venue mapping, route security and pre-event readiness — first in, last out.

Perimeter

Access Control

Secures entrances and exits; verifies every tag and wristband.

Venue

Ground Control

Auditorium safety, lost-and-found, first point of contact for attendees.

Commerce

Market Control

Vendor compliance and dispute resolution around market areas.

Movement

Traffic

Vehicle flow, parking and coordination with local traffic authorities.

Support

Management & Welfare

Hydration, first aid, and daily equipment issue and retrieval.

Reinforcement

All Personnel

Patrols and protects security members; investigates theft, assault and fraud.

Uniform Standard

Dressed for the post.

Every Flaming Security volunteer on duty is identifiable, professional and ready. The uniform is not decoration — it is accountability made visible.

YMR 2026 Standard
Uniform Specification

2026 Deployment Standard

  • ShirtDepartment-issued navy shirt with Flaming Security logo and unit designation
  • TrousersNavy cargo with gold side stripe — pressed and clean at every call-up
  • FootwearBlack boots, polished — closed toe mandatory for all active posts
  • ID BadgeIssued by Welfare Unit each deployment, worn on left chest at all times
  • PatchesUnit designation on right shoulder; department crest on left
  • BeltEmber-orange service belt — worn at all times on active post
  • WristbandColour-coded per deployment for zone identification
Uniform rule: Volunteers report in full clean uniform at least 20–30 minutes before call-up. Late arrivals or incorrect uniform result in exclusion from that session's service.
